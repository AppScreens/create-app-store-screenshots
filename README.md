# AppScreens: App Store and Google Play Screenshot Generator

[AppScreens](https://appscreens.com/) turns real app captures into editable screenshots for the App Store and Google Play. Add captions that explain why your app is useful, make the design your own, and prepare the sizes and languages you need for release.

Start free with a template or a blank layout. Upload your app screens, edit captions and design, then preview and export the sizes you need. You can also upload screenshots to connected stores on a supported plan. Keep the editable project ready for your next release.

## Why use AppScreens instead of building a screenshot pipeline?

Use AppScreens to produce the screenshot set, from the first design to store delivery. It provides the layouts, device frames, store sizes, text fitting and reusable project that your team or agent would otherwise need to build and maintain in HTML, canvas or scripts.

- **Create the first set.** Start with a template or blank layout, add your real app captures and use benefit-led captions to explain the app. Start free with a small launch set.
- **Produce the selected combinations.** Adapt the design across devices and languages. Fit translated captions, assign local captures, preview the results and export the chosen sizes together.
- **Keep control of the next release.** Work on the same editable project through your agent or the visual editor. Replace changed screens and regenerate the selected outputs from the saved project.

## Make your first screenshot set

1. **Start with your app and its benefits.** Find your app and import its store listing details, or add context for an unpublished app. Decide what benefit each screenshot should show.
2. **Choose a template or start blank.** Pick a composition from the [screenshot templates](https://appscreens.com/templates), or build your own. Set the order so the first screenshot introduces the main benefit and the rest show how it works.
3. **Add real captures and make the design yours.** Upload your app screens. Edit the captions, fonts, colors, backgrounds and device frames. Use panoramic layouts, portrait or landscape designs, and your own images where they fit the story.

## Adapt your screenshots for devices and languages

Create **Apple App Store and Google Play screenshots from one project**. Select the outputs your app supports for **iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro, Android phones and tablets**. Supply the appropriate captures for each device. AppScreens adapts the design across the selected sizes, with additional presets and custom dimensions for other assets.

Use the website's [Apple screenshot sizes](https://appscreens.com/app-store-screenshot-sizes) and [Google Play screenshot sizes](https://appscreens.com/google-play-screenshot-sizes) guides as the reference for dimensions and store requirements.

[Localize screenshot captions](https://appscreens.com/localize-app-store-screenshots), use different app captures for each language and adjust the result in the editor. Automatic text fitting, right-to-left text and alignment, and per-language fonts help adapt the design to different scripts and caption lengths. Preview each version to check readability and cropping.

## Preview, export and upload

Review the selected devices and languages in AppScreens, then check how your screenshots look in the [App Store listing preview](https://appscreens.com/app-store-screenshot-preview) and [Google Play listing preview](https://appscreens.com/google-play-screenshot-preview). Check that captions fit, the right captures are assigned and the screenshot order tells the story you intended.

Download your screenshots as PNG or JPEG files. On supported plans, connect your store accounts and [upload screenshots to App Store Connect and Google Play](https://appscreens.com/upload-app-store-screenshots). Choose the app, destination, sizes and languages before sending the assets.

Uploading screenshots does not submit or publish your app.

## Keep the project ready for your next release

When your app changes, replace the affected captures, update the captions and export the new set from the saved project. Keep the branding and layout you already approved.

You can also prepare alternative first screenshots, messages and page orders for store experiments. AppScreens creates the assets. Run the experiment and measure results using your store's testing tools.

For the rest of your listing, create matching [Google Play feature graphics](https://appscreens.com/google-play-feature-graphic-generator) and manage [store listing copy and metadata](https://appscreens.com/app-store-manager). The metadata workflow lets you export JSON, edit it with your preferred tools, import the changes and review them before saving to the store.

## Connect your AI assistant or release workflow

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) connects Claude Code, Claude Desktop and web, Codex Desktop and CLI, and Cursor to editable AppScreens projects. ChatGPT users can follow the custom MCP plugin setup for supported workspaces.

The examples above were rendered from editable AppScreens projects. Follow the [complete Codex walkthrough](https://appscreens.com/blog/create-app-store-screenshots-codex), or use the [prompt builder](https://appscreens.com/app-store-screenshots-mcp#mcp-first-prompt) to start with your own app.

1. **Connect using the website instructions.** Choose your client in the [connection guide](https://appscreens.com/developers/connect). Install the AppScreens plugin where supported, or add the hosted MCP server, then sign in to AppScreens and approve access.
2. **Add the skills if needed.** The plugin includes the AppScreens skills. For a manual installation, follow the [skills page](https://appscreens.com/developers/skills). The skills guide the workflow, and MCP supplies the tools.
3. **Check your connection.** Use the prompt "Check my AppScreens connection and tell me what you can do with my account." This reads setup, capabilities and usage before making changes. See the [API and MCP reference](https://appscreens.com/developers/reference) for supported operations.
4. **Give the agent your app and the job.** Supply captures, a project or template, and the required stores, devices and languages. Follow the [workflow guides](https://appscreens.com/developers/guides), review the rendered previews and approve store delivery separately.

**[Build your AppScreens prompt](https://appscreens.com/app-store-screenshots-mcp#mcp-first-prompt)**

Add your app or idea, choose a reference listing or template, and select your screenshot count, output sizes and languages. Tell the builder whether your app captures are ready, then copy the generated prompt into your AI assistant. The prompt includes the AppScreens workflow and connection instructions.

The [AppScreens skill repository](https://github.com/AppScreens/app-store-screenshots-skill) is the distribution source for the official skills.

For scripts and CI, follow [REST setup](https://appscreens.com/developers/connect#rest-connection) and the [release automation guide](https://appscreens.com/developers/guides#automate-releases). See [troubleshooting](https://appscreens.com/developers/help) for connection, permission and operation errors.

## Common questions

### Do I need AI to use AppScreens?

No. You can create the entire set in the visual editor. Guided AI is optional, and AppScreens projects created with guided AI remain editable.

### Can I use captures from a simulator or my existing release tools?

Yes. Bring captures from your device, iOS Simulator, Android Emulator or a capture workflow such as fastlane. AppScreens adds the screenshot design, captions and store outputs around those captures.

### Can I start for free?

Yes. Start with a free project for your own app. See [current pricing and plan limits](https://appscreens.com/pricing) for project allowances, localization and direct uploads. For paid client work, check the [licensing options](https://appscreens.com/licence).

This repository introduces the hosted AppScreens editor and developer workflows. The website is the reference for setup, supported operations and workflow details.
